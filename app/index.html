0/550% to platinum
⚠ One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude — meditate on the Chapter 1 balcony before Chapter 1 ends. Every other trophy can be earned in free-roam after the story.

One playthrough, about 200 hours, no online trophies, no mission replay — so the order matters. Play the story first (stages 1–2), finish the side strands (stage 3), then clean up robberies, activities and collectibles in free-roam (stages 4–6) before the 100% sweep (stage 7). Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them alone.

Missables — what can actually go wrong

GTA VI has no mission replay, so this page is the one to read before you start. The good news: only one trophy is missable, and everything else — including every 100% checklist item — stays available in free-roam after the story.

Safe after the story

  • Every quest giver becomes available again in free-roam, even ones who vanish for chapters (Kenton, for example).
  • Optional missions with hourglass icons can fail, but none of them gate a trophy — there are 200–300+ optional missions and the 100% checklist needs only 100.
  • Some fences and magazine collectibles in story buildings disappear, but replacements exist elsewhere and no trophy needs them.
  • Strangers & Freaks questlines stay open in free-roam.

Sources: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap; Exophase — GTA 6 Trophies (official list).

100% Checklist — the real numbers

A Long Road needs the 100% checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion), unlocked after the story. It is closer to 60% true completion — most side quests and collectibles are only partly required. These are the counts that matter.
